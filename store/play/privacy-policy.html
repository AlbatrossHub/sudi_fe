Privacy Policy — Sudi & Sudi Work apps

SUPER DIAMOND PROCESSORS PRIVATE LIMITED (“SDPPL”, “we”) · Mumbai, India · Effective 7 November 2026 · Applies to the mobile applications Sudi (for customers) and Sudi Work (for SDPPL staff). The website policy lives at sdppl.com/privacy-policy.

1. Who the apps are for

Sudi lets our customers photograph a jangad (issue voucher), choose the diamond services they need, and arrange a pickup. Anyone may sign up with their mobile number. Sudi Work is used only by SDPPL employees; accounts are created by the company and there is no public sign-up.

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no sale of data, no sharing with data brokers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Data is shared outside SDPPL only with service providers that run our infrastructure (hosting, WhatsApp message delivery, push notifications) and when the law requires it.

4. Security and retention

All traffic between the apps and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Data stored on the phone is encrypted on the device. Business records are kept as long as Indian commercial and tax law requires; account data is kept while your account exists.

5. Deleting your account and data

In the Sudi app: Account → Delete my account. You can also request deletion without the app at sdppl.com/app/delete-account. Deletion closes the account and removes personal data not required for legal record-keeping. Staff accounts in Sudi Work are managed by the company and end with employment.

6. Permissions the apps ask for

  • Camera / photos — only when you photograph or pick a jangad page, a receipt page or a signature.
  • Location (Sudi Work only) — only while recording a pickup or delivery.
  • Notifications — order and work updates. Optional.
