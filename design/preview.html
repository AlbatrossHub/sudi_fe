Neumorphic screens for the two Flutter flavors

Drawn against docs/FLUTTER_INTEGRATION_BRIEF.md and the two frozen OpenAPI documents. Every screen names the call behind it. Scroll each row sideways.

The design rule that runs through all of it: depth is carried by shadow, state is never carried by shadow. Queued, synced, someone-else and blocked are saturated chips with real contrast, because a soft neumorphic shadow disappears in Surat daylight.
