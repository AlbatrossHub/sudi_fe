Delete your SDPPL account

For the SDPPL app by SUPER DIAMOND PROCESSORS PRIVATE LIMITED.

In the app (fastest)

Open SDPPL → tap Account (top right) → Delete my account → confirm. Your account closes immediately.

Without the app

Send the request from the mobile number the account uses, so we can verify it is yours:

  • WhatsApp or call: +91 80808 09288
  • Email: info@sdppl.com — include the account’s mobile number.

We confirm and close the account within 7 days.

What is deleted, what is kept

  • Deleted: your sign-in, registered phones and push tokens, and personal details not needed for legal records.
  • Kept: jangads, deliveries and invoices that are part of our business and tax records, for the period Indian law requires. They are no longer linked to an active account.
